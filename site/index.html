Haul — a download manager for macOS

Haul is a free, open-source download manager for Mac. It downloads files faster over several connections at once, pauses and resumes them (even after a restart), and keeps them in a queue you can reorder.

  • Multi-connection downloads with pause and resume
  • A queue with a limit on simultaneous downloads, a speed limit and a nightly schedule
  • Proxies, automatic retries, and fresh links for expired downloads
  • Sorting into folders by kind, SHA-256 checksums, and extracting archives when they finish
  • Progress in the menu bar and Dock, and notifications
  • Extensions for Safari, Chrome and Firefox that hand your browser's downloads to Haul, plus the Share menu, Services and a haul command
